# Sample Wealth Platform FAQ (fictional)

## Account statements

Monthly account statements are emailed on the 5th of every month. Past statements for the last 24
months can be downloaded from the Documents section after login.

## KYC update

To update KYC details, upload a valid proof of address in the Profile section. Verification usually
takes 2 to 3 working days. Investments stay active during verification.

## SIP changes

A systematic investment plan can be paused, modified or cancelled from the SIP section at least 3
working days before the next instalment date.

## Redemption timeline

Redemption requests placed before 3 pm are processed the same working day. Funds usually reach the
registered bank account within 1 to 3 working days, depending on the fund type.

## Fees and charges

Platform fees are listed on the Pricing page. Fund-level expense ratios are shown on each fund's
factsheet and are deducted by the fund house, not by the platform.

## Login and password

Use the Forgot Password link on the login page. A reset link is sent to the registered email and
expires after 30 minutes.
