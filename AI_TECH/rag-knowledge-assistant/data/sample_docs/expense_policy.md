# Expense Policy (sample, fictional company)

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through the company travel desk. Economy class applies to flights under 6
hours. Business class needs written approval from a director. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 8,000
rupees per night in metro cities and 5,000 rupees elsewhere.

## Meals and entertainment

Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 1,500 rupees per day. Client entertainment needs prior
approval from the department head, and receipts must list every attendee.

## Claim submission

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense, with original receipts attached. Claims above
25,000 rupees need approval from the finance controller. Approved claims are paid with the next
payroll run.

## Not reimbursable

Personal travel, fines and penalties, alcohol outside approved client events, and expenses without
receipts are not reimbursable.
