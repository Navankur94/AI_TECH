# Information Security Policy (sample, fictional company)

## Passwords and access

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and rotated every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication
is mandatory for email, the VPN and the production console. Shared accounts are not allowed.

## Devices

Company laptops use full-disk encryption and lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lost or stolen devices
must be reported to the security desk within 2 hours so the device can be remotely wiped.

## Incident reporting

Report suspected phishing, malware or data exposure to the security team by email or the security
hotline. The security team acknowledges reports within 1 hour and classifies severity within 4 hours.

## Customer data

Customer personal data may only be accessed on a need-to-know basis. Exporting customer data to
personal devices or personal cloud storage is prohibited. Access to production data is logged and
reviewed every quarter.
